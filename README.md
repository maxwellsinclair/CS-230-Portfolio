# CS 230 Portfolio Reflection

### Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?
The Gaming Room wanted to expand their game, Draw It or Lose It, from an Android application into a web based game that could work on different devices. The game needed to support multiple players and teams while keeping track of games, scores, and unique names.

### What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?
I think I did well explaining the different operating systems and comparing their advantages and disadvantages. I also made sure my recommendations were easy to understand and related to what the client needed.

### What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?
Having the design document helped me understand how the different parts of the game should work together before writing the code. The UML diagram was especially helpful because it showed how the classes were connected and what each one was responsible for.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
I would probably improve the operating systems comparison by adding more detail about the costs and differences between each platform. This would make it easier to understand why one operating system might be better than another.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?
I focused on making sure the game could work on different devices while supporting multiple players and teams. I also considered security, storage, and performance when making my recommendations. Understanding what the client needs is important because it helps make sure the final application works the way they expect.

### How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?
I started by looking at the client's requirements and figuring out what the application needed to do. From there, I used the UML diagram and design document to organize the different parts of the game. In the future, I would use a similar approach by planning the design first and comparing different options before starting development.
